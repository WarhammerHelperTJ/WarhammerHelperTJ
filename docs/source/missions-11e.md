# Warhammer 40,000 (11th edition) Dice Helper — Missions
## Chapter Approved 2026-27 Mission Deck: Primary and Secondary Missions

Data captured from Wahapedia (wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/mission-deck-2026-27/) on 15 November 2026. Flavour text left out; rules text verbatim.

How a battle is scored (Chapter Approved battles):
- Each detachment has a Force Disposition (one, or two to choose from). Each player reveals one; your Primary Mission is the card for your Force Disposition against your opponent's (header: "mine vs opponent's").
- Secondary Missions: each player uses Tactical (draw two at the start of your Command phase; once per battle at the end of your Command phase, spend 1CP to discard one and draw one) or two Fixed cards (marked "(Fixed)", never discarded).
- At the end of each player's turn, each player (starting with the player whose turn it is) can gain the VP of each Secondary Mission whose conditions they met; a Tactical card that scores is discarded (achieved). Then, in your own turn, you can discard Tactical cards: gain 1CP.
- The battle ends after five battle rounds. Battle Ready army: 10VP.
- Maximum VP: Primary Mission 45VP (up to 15VP per battle round); Secondary Missions 45VP (up to 15VP per battle round), and up to 20VP per Fixed Secondary Mission card; Battle Ready 10VP.

Card format: "### <battle rounds>" block, "WHEN: <timing>" for the lines below it, "• <VP> — <condition>". "OR" = alternative to the line above; "CUMULATIVE" = adds to the line above; "(UP TO nVP)" = cap for that line; "FIXED"/"TACTICAL" = VP depends on how the card is used.
